Privacy notice for applicants

Mock Interviews GmbH is a training simulation for AI master's students. It is not a registered company and not a real employer. This notice explains what happens with the data you send through the application form.

1. Who is responsible

Tharun Kumar Korine Palli, who runs the mock interviews. Contact: .

2. What data we collect

  • Your contact details: name, email address, phone number and city.
  • Your education, links to LinkedIn or GitHub if you add them, and your answers in the form.
  • The documents you upload: CV, cover letter and optional certificates.
  • Your chosen interview slot and the time you applied.

Please do not upload a photo, date of birth or other details that the interview does not need.

3. Why we use it

Only to run your mock interview: to prepare questions, hold the interview, and give you feedback. Your data is not shared with employers or anyone else, and it is not used to make automated decisions.

4. Legal basis

Your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you give when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time by email. Withdrawal does not affect anything done before it.

5. Where the data is stored

Your application is sent over an encrypted connection to a small server function at Cloudflare, Inc. It passes the data straight on and does not keep a copy. The data is stored in a private GitHub repository (GitHub, Inc.) that only the person named in section 1 can access. Both providers may process data in the United States. They are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

This website does not use cookies, tracking or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs technical access data such as IP addresses for security.

6. How long we keep it

We delete your application no later than 6 months after your interview, or earlier if you ask us to.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict its use, to receive a copy, and to withdraw your consent. Write to the contact address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA).
