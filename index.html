Our hiring process

Short, clear and the same for every role.

1

Apply online

Send your CV, cover letter and short answers to three role questions. Takes about 20 minutes.

2

Book your slot

Each role has its own interview weekend. You choose a Saturday or Sunday slot while you apply.

3

Technical interview

A 45 minute video interview with our CEO: your projects, the fundamentals for the role, and one practical case.

4

Feedback

You get structured feedback on your CV, your answers and the interview, so you know what to improve.

About Mock Interviews GmbH

Mock Interviews GmbH runs realistic hiring for AI roles. Every job description on this page is built from real job ads that companies in Germany and Europe published in 2026. The skills, tools and questions are the ones employers ask for right now.

We hire for seven teams: Applied AI, Perception, LLM Platform, Applied AI Research, Applied Science, Central AI and ML Platform. Each team interviews on its own weekend, two weeks apart, so you can prepare for one role at a time.

Mock Interviews GmbH is a training simulation for AI master's students. It is not a real employer and no employment contract results from an application. Your data is used only to run and evaluate your mock interview.
